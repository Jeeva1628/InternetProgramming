Online Bus Ticket Booking System

Saveetha School of Engineering | Department of Computer Science and Engineering | CSA43: Internet Programming

Project Overview

The online bus ticket booking system enables users to search for buses by route, date, and time, and view available seats. Users can create an account, log in, and manage their bookings through a personal dashboard. The booking process includes selecting seats, filling out passenger details, and making secure payments via an integrated payment gateway.

Admins can manage bus schedules, seat availability, and user accounts through an admin panel. Users receive booking confirmation emails and can view their booking history. The website is mobile-responsive, ensuring easy access on all devices.

Final Output in XAMPP

  • Admin can maintain all bus information and bus categories.
  • Admin can manage registered users and news.
  • Admin can generate reports and manage the entire system.
